Significant Figures
===================

Controls the number of significant figures to display for numerical values in the output.

Usage:
- Enter a positive integer (e.g., 3, 4, 5)
- Leave blank to keep original precision
- Applies to all numeric fields being extracted

Examples:
- "3" - Display numbers like 1.23, 12.3, 123
- "4" - Display numbers like 1.234, 12.34, 123.4
- "5" - Display numbers like 1.2345, 12.345, 123.45

Technical Details:
- Uses significant figures, not decimal places
- Counts all non-zero digits and zeros between non-zero digits
- Scientific notation is used for very large or small numbers

Tips:
- Match the precision of your source data or measurement equipment
- More figures = more storage space and potentially false precision
- Consider the context: electrical specs may need 3-4, mechanical may need 2-3
- Use with "Rounding Tolerance" for intelligent rounding
